# Doubt

Ordinary work uses Council. Doubt is for decisions you cannot cheaply undo, or that could hurt someone if you are confidently wrong.

## The card

```
Claim:
Why we believe it (evidence, not adjectives):
Best objection:
What would change our mind:
Narrower claim we can defend today:
```

Write the objection in the voice of a careful skeptic, not a heckler. If you cannot think of an objection, you have not understood the claim. Ask `fullspan-scout` for one disconfirming source before you proceed.

## Then

- If the objection wins, change the promise. Do not "note the risk" and ship the original claim.
- If the evidence answers the objection, say which fact answered it.
- If neither, ship the narrower claim. A smaller true thing beats a large hopeful one.
- Get a second context (a cold reader, or the user) when the cost of being wrong is borne by someone else.

## Always doubt

- Deleting or migrating data others depend on.
- A security or privacy boundary becoming looser.
- Instructions a person might follow in the physical world (doses, wiring, legal steps). Refuse to invent those. Point at a qualified source or stop.
- A public claim about a real person or organization you did not verify.

## Never doubt as delay

A reversible slice with a green check does not need a card. Doubt is a gate, not a personality.
